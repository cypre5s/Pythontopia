## Thumbnail / content description
13 condensed 320x200 town tilesets at 20x20 tile size, covering 4 seasons (spring/summer/autumn/winter) crossed with 2 daytimes (day/night), plus matching 4-season cave tilesets and an indoor tileset. 256-color palette, BMP format.
