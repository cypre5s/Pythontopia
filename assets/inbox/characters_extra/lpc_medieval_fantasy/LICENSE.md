## How OGA-BY 3.0 protects Pythontopia

- OGA-BY 3.0 is **not ShareAlike**: derivative works (Pythontopia game code, our extra recolours, our combined kits) do **not** need to be released under OGA-BY 3.0 or any specific licence.
- OGA-BY 3.0 explicitly **permits** consumers to relicense to **CC-BY 3.0** if they need a non-OGA upstream legal citation; for academic / class use this is usually simpler.
- We may freely combine these sprite layers with our own original LPC-style overlays.

## Why we did NOT take the LPC base assets (Redshrike) directly

The upstream LPC base assets at https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles are only released under **CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0** in their structured licence field — they do **not** include OGA-BY 3.0. They were excluded from the R-100 whitelist because SA is viral. wulax's pack here is the only LPC-format walk + idle kit we could include legally.

## Forbidden uses

- Do **not** combine these sheets with any CC-BY-SA-only LPC layer (e.g. the upstream Redshrike base) inside Pythontopia. If a derived layer is needed, regenerate it from these wulax kits (which already include `BODY_male.png` / `BODY_skeleton.png` 9-frame walk + 1-frame idle rows) or paint a new layer ourselves under our own licence.
- Do **not** ship the bundled `.xcf` Gimp source files (already excluded from the inbox copy; they remain in the working `.work/R-100/assets_dl/characters_extra/lpc_medieval_fantasy/` directory only).
