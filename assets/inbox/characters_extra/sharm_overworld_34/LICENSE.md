## Notes on transparency

The downloaded `batch_one.png` is **RGB** (no alpha channel). The author used a uniform background colour (typically magenta `#ff00ff` or hot pink — verify against the actual PNG in your asset pipeline) as the transparent key. Pythontopia's asset pipeline must convert the magenta key to alpha-zero before runtime use; the original RGB PNG is preserved in this folder unchanged for traceability.
