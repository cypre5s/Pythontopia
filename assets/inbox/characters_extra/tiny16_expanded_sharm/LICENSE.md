## Why this is the right LPC-compatible base for student/teacher faces

Sharm is also the author of the earlier `assets/inbox/characters/tiny16_sharm/characters_1.png` (Tiny 16 Basic) in this repo, so visual style and palette are consistent between the two packs. This "Expanded" submission adds **male / female / skeleton / nude base** variants that the original basic pack lacks, which is exactly what we need for the Pythontopia "多种发型 / 肤色 / 校服" requirement.
